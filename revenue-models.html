A BUSINESS PERSPECTIVE

Revenue is not the same as profitability.

A programme may generate substantial fees and still be commercially weak if learner acquisition is expensive, delivery costs are high, partner shares are poorly negotiated or enrolment is inconsistent. Understanding the model means understanding both the income and the economics behind it.
